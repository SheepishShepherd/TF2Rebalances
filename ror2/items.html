Getting a bit of gold out of being hit doesn't account for much, especially when taking damage is something most survivors want to avoid. Changing the condition to activating an interactable limits the amount of times it can be used, but offers cheaper chest costs in the form of rebates.

Leeching Seed
Killing an enemy gives you a burst of movement speed.
18% chance to leech an enemy for 4 (+2 per stack) seconds on hit. Killing a leeched enemy heals for 8% of your maximum health.
Changes:
> No longer heals when dealing damage.
> Attacks now have a chance to leech enemies. Killing a leeched enemy heals you.
Notes:
Leech is a stackable debuff, but the application of a new stack does not refresh the duration of any current stacks. Every stack of leech heals for +8% of maximum health when the enemy is killed. Upon the death of a leeched enemy, a red healing orb will hover over the death location for a brief period, then start to home in towards the killer and heal them.
